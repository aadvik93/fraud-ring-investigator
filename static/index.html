🔗 Multi-hop Cypher (FalkorDB)

MATCH (a:Account {id: 'RING_B_1'})-[r:SENT*2..6]->(a)
RETURN [rel IN r | startNode(rel).id] AS cycle_nodes,
       reduce(total = 0.0, rel IN r | total + rel.amount) AS total_amount

⚡ FalkorDB runs this in milliseconds using sparse matrix multiplication (GraphBLAS). The equivalent SQL needs a recursive CTE with cycle-detection guards — tens of lines of code, and degrades exponentially as the graph grows. Fraud patterns like "find all accounts that share a device AND appear in a money cycle" are natural in Cypher, unnatural in SQL.
